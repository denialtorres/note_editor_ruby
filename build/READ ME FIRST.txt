Note Editor — installing

1. Drag "Note Editor" onto the "Applications" folder next to it.
2. Eject this disk image.
3. Open Note Editor from Applications or Launchpad.

First launch

The app is not notarized with Apple, so macOS will refuse to open it the
first time ("Apple could not verify..."). To allow it once:

  a. Click "Done" on that message.
  b. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click
     "Open Anyway" next to the Note Editor message. Confirm.

Or, in Terminal:

  xattr -cr "/Applications/Note Editor.app"

After that it opens normally.

Notes

- The editor opens in a Google Chrome window without browser chrome. Brave,
  Edge, Chromium, or Vivaldi work too. Without any of them it falls back to
  your default browser.
- Closing the window quits the app. Your notes are stored in
  ~/Library/Application Support/Note Editor/notes.sqlite3
- Works on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs, macOS 12 or newer.
